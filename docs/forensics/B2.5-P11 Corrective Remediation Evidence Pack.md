## 8. Final signature-hash definition

`artifact_hash = compute_artifact_hash(length_framed_artifact_identity_bytes)`.

`signature_material = b"skeldir:b25-p11:export-artifact:v1\x00" + artifact_hash.encode("ascii")`.

`signature_hash = sha256_tagged(signature_material)` through P2 `compute_detached_signature_hash(bytes)`.

`signature = Ed25519.sign(signature_material)`.

Thus `signature_hash` and the Ed25519 signature identify/authenticate the same canonical signed bytes. It is neither `artifact_hash` nor a hash of textual signature output.
